ABOUT SAAJ

Designed for
the way you live.

SAAJ INTERIORS is a Mumbai-based interior design studio creating spaces that feel sophisticated, warm and unmistakably personal.

We believe beautiful interiors are not simply about decoration. They are about proportion, light, texture, functionality and the small details that make a space feel like home.

FOUNDER & DESIGNER SAMRUDDHI

MEET THE FOUNDER

Designing spaces
with soul.

SAAJ INTERIORS is led by Samruddhi, a passionate interior designer with a vision to create spaces that feel elegant, personal and timeless.

For Samruddhi, interior design is more than choosing beautiful furniture or colours. It is about understanding the people behind a space and creating an environment that reflects their personality, lifestyle and dreams.

Her design approach brings together functionality, comfort and refined aesthetics. Every material, texture, colour and detail is thoughtfully selected to create interiors that feel naturally beautiful.
